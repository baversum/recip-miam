## 1. Overview

A shortcrust pastry tart filled with almond frangipane and thinly sliced pears arranged in a rosette. A festive dessert, served warm or at room temperature, optionally dusted with icing sugar. 22 to 24 cm tin, makes 6 to 8 slices. Preparation: 40 minutes. Baking: 40 to 45 minutes. The pastry and frangipane can both be made the day before without any loss of quality.

## 2. Ingredients

### Shortcrust pastry

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Flour | 200 g |
| Cold unsalted butter | 100 g |
| Egg | 1 |
| Sugar | 1 pinch |
| Salt | 1 pinch |

A store-bought pure butter shortcrust pastry works as a replacement.

### Frangipane

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ground almonds | 100 g |
| Soft unsalted butter | 80 g |
| Sugar | 80 g |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Bitter almond extract or rum | 1 tsp or 1 tbsp (optional) |

### Topping and finishing

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ripe pears (Conference type) | 4 |
| Icing sugar | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 tart tin, 22 to 24 cm
- 1 whisk or spatula
- 1 fork
- 1 paring knife

## 4. Shortcrust pastry

1. Rub the flour, cold diced butter, salt and sugar between your fingertips until the mixture is sandy.
2. Add the egg and bring together into a ball without overworking the dough.
3. Smear the dough once or twice with the palm of your hand, shape it into a disc, wrap in film.
4. Rest in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, ideally overnight.

## 5. Frangipane

1. Cream the soft butter and sugar until homogeneous.
2. Add the ground almonds, then the eggs one by one.
3. Flavour with bitter almond extract or rum.
4. The frangipane keeps overnight in the fridge in a closed container.

## 6. Assembly

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F).
2. Line the tin with the pastry and prick the base with a fork.
3. Spread the frangipane over the pastry base.
4. Peel the pears, slice them thinly and remove the core.
5. Arrange the slices in a rosette over the frangipane, pressing them in lightly.

## 7. Baking

1. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes at 180 °C (350 °F).
2. The tart is done when the frangipane is puffed and golden and the pastry is cooked underneath.
3. Let it cool on a rack for at least 1 hour: the frangipane sets and the tart cuts cleanly.

## 8. Serving and storage

- Serve warm or at room temperature, dusted with icing sugar.
- Storage: 48 hours at room temperature under a cake dome, 4 days in the fridge.
- Optional reheating: 10 minutes at 150 °C (300 °F).

## 9. Variations

- Pears poached for 10 minutes in a light syrup, for extra softness.
- Fresh apricots in season instead of pears.
- A few flaked almonds scattered on top before baking.
