## 1. Overview

A flourless banana bread structured by oat flakes: banana and Greek yoghurt bring the moistness, cocoa and cinnamon the flavour. A short bake at 200 °C gives a well-marked crust and a still-fudgy centre. Makes 6 slices. Preparation: 10 minutes. Baking: 30 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rolled oats | 50 g |
| Ripe banana | 1 |
| Greek yoghurt | 150 g |
| Egg | 1 |
| Cinnamon | 1/2 tsp |
| Unsweetened cocoa powder | 20 g |
| Maple syrup | 20 g |
| Baking powder | 1/2 tsp |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 bowl
- 1 fork
- 1 lined loaf tin

## 4. Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Mash the banana with a fork, add the egg and yoghurt, mix.
3. Fold in the oats, cocoa, cinnamon and baking powder, then the maple syrup.
4. Rest the batter 10 minutes so the flakes hydrate.
5. Pour into the tin and bake 30 minutes at 200 °C, until a knife blade comes out clean.

## 5. Key points

- The flakes must be well hydrated before baking, otherwise the centre stays dry.
- At 200 °C the crust browns fast: check from 25 minutes depending on the tin.
- A narrow loaf tin gives a dense crumb: a wider tin bakes faster.

## 6. Variations

- A handful of chocolate chips in the batter.
- Chopped walnuts for crunch.
- A banana sliced on top before baking.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve warm or toasted.
- Storage: 3 days in the fridge.
- Reheating: 2 minutes in the toaster or 20 seconds in the microwave.
