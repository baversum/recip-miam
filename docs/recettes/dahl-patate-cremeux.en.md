## 1. Overview

A dhal of red lentils and melting potatoes, bound with coconut milk. The crushed tomatoes bring acidity and the lemon juice, added at the end, lifts the roundness of the coconut. A complete vegetarian one-pot dish. Makes 5 portions. Preparation: 15 minutes. Cooking: 30 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 15 ml (1 tbsp) |
| Cumin seeds | 1 tsp |
| Onion | 1 |
| Garlic | 3 cloves |
| Grated fresh ginger | 1 tsp |
| Curry powder | 2 tsp |
| Crushed tomatoes (1 can) | 400 g |
| Potatoes | 800 g |
| Chicken stock | 700 ml |
| Red lentils | 200 g |
| Coconut milk | 400 ml |
| Lemons | 3 (juice) |
| Fresh coriander (cilantro) | a few sprigs |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 large Dutch oven or stockpot
- 1 board and 1 knife
- 1 fine grater for the ginger

## 4. Preparation

1. Heat the oil in the pot over medium heat. Cook the sliced onion and cumin seeds for 3 minutes, without colouring.
2. Add the chopped garlic and grated ginger, cook 1 minute, then the curry powder for 30 seconds, stirring.
3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, stir, then add the potatoes in 2 cm chunks and the stock.
4. Bring to the boil, lower the heat and simmer 12 to 15 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
5. Add the red lentils and coconut milk, cook 10 to 12 minutes over low heat, stirring regularly, until the lentils have melted and thickened the dhal.
6. Off the heat, add the lemon juice, salt and pepper. Adjust the seasoning.
7. Scatter chopped coriander over when serving.

## 5. Key points

- Red lentils catch on the bottom at the end of cooking: stir regularly for the last 10 minutes.
- The lemon juice goes in off the boil: its aroma evaporates if cooked.
- The dhal thickens a lot as it rests: keep some hot water for reheating.

## 6. Variations

- A handful of fresh spinach stirred in at the end of cooking.
- A fresh chilli chopped with the garlic for a spicier dhal.
- Sweet potatoes instead of potatoes.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve with basmati rice or naan bread, scattered with coriander.
- Storage: 4 days in the fridge in an airtight container.
- Reheating: over low heat with a splash of water, 5 minutes.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months.
