## 1. Overview

A fudgy flourless brownie: mashed banana and petits suisses replace butter and flour, cocoa brings the chocolate intensity. Baked in the Cake Factory sharing-cakes programme, 45 minutes. Makes 6 to 8 pieces. Preparation: 15 minutes. Baking: 45 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ripe bananas | 4 |
| Eggs | 4 |
| Petits suisses (small fresh cheese pots) | 4 |
| Unsweetened cocoa powder | 30 g |
| Maple syrup | 30 to 50 g depending on banana ripeness |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 bowl
- 1 fork or stick blender
- 1 Cake Factory sharing-cake tin (or a 20 to 22 cm round tin in the oven)

## 4. Preparation

1. Mash the bananas with a fork into a smooth purée.
2. Add the eggs and petits suisses, mix until homogeneous.
3. Fold in the sifted cocoa and baking powder, then the maple syrup. Mix without overworking.
4. Pour into the tin and smooth the surface.
5. Bake 45 minutes in the Cake Factory sharing-cakes programme (or 45 minutes at 180 °C in the oven), until a knife blade comes out barely moist.

## 5. Key points

- The riper the bananas, the less maple syrup is needed: start with 30 g.
- The brownie keeps setting as it cools: the centre can stay slightly soft out of the oven.
- Without flour, the texture is dense and fudgy, not crackly like a classic brownie.

## 6. Variations

- A handful of dark chocolate chips in the batter.
- Chopped walnuts or hazelnuts scattered on top before baking.
- 1 tsp instant coffee dissolved in the batter to deepen the cocoa.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve warm or at room temperature.
- Storage: 4 days in the fridge wrapped in film.
- Freezes well in portions, thaw at room temperature.
