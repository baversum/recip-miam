## 1. Overview

A vegetarian curry where red lentils simmer in a vegetable broth flavoured with curry and cumin, bound by crushed tomatoes and softened with coconut milk. Red lentils melt as they cook and naturally thicken the sauce, without any liaison or blending; frozen spinach is added at the end of cooking and releases its water at just the right time. The spices are bloomed in butter before the liquids go in, which develops their aromas. Serves 4. Preparation: 10 minutes. Cooking: 30 minutes. Served with rice.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Red lentils | 300 g |
| Frozen spinach | 300 g |
| Crushed tomatoes | 400 g |
| Coconut milk | 200 ml |
| Onion | 1 |
| Garlic | 3 cloves |
| Curry powder | 4 tbsp |
| Cumin | 3 tbsp |
| Espelette pepper | 1 tsp |
| Water + vegetable stock | 600 ml |
| Butter | for cooking |
| Salt | to taste |
| Rice | to serve |

The butter can be replaced with olive oil. The spice amounts (4 tbsp curry, 3 tbsp cumin) are generous and give a very fragrant curry; Espelette pepper can be replaced with cayenne, halving the amount.

## 3. Equipment

- 1 large pot or stockpot
- 1 sieve for rinsing the lentils
- 1 saucepan for the rice

## 4. Preparation

1. Rinse the red lentils in clear water until it runs clear. Slice the onion and chop the garlic.
2. Melt a knob of butter in the pot over medium heat. Cook the onion 3 to 4 minutes until translucent, then add the garlic for 1 more minute.
3. Add the curry powder, cumin and Espelette pepper, stir for 1 minute so the spices release their aromas.
4. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, the rinsed lentils and the 600 ml of water with the vegetable stock. Bring to the boil.
5. Lower the heat and simmer 20 to 25 minutes, stirring regularly: the lentils melt and thicken the curry.
6. Add the frozen spinach and the coconut milk, cook 5 more minutes.
7. Salt to taste. Serve with rice.

## 5. Key points

- Stir regularly while simmering: the lentils catch on the bottom at the end of cooking.
- Do not salt before adding the spinach: it releases water that dilutes the seasoning.
- The coconut milk goes in at the end, with the spinach: boiled for a long time it can split.
- The texture is adjusted at the end of cooking: too thick, add a little hot water; too thin, reduce a few minutes uncovered.

## 6. Variations

- Fresh grated ginger added with the garlic.
- Replace about 100 g of lentils with a dried mixed bean blend (white and red beans, soy), soaked overnight and cooked 45 minutes beforehand.
- Finishing touch when serving: fresh coriander or a squeeze of lemon juice.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve hot, with rice.
- Reheating: 3 minutes in the microwave with 1 tbsp of water, stirring halfway, or over low heat in a pan with a splash of water.
- Storage: 3 to 4 days in the fridge. The curry thickens as it cools, keep some water for reheating.
- Also good cold, in a salad or as a wrap filling, for a picnic or a road meal without reheating.
