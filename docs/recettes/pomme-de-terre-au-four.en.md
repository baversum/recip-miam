## 1. Overview

Stuffed potatoes filled with their own flesh mixed with fresh garlic-and-herb cheese (Boursin type), gratinated in the oven. The microwave pre-cooking cuts the preparation time drastically compared to a fully oven-baked version.

**Serves 4** — Preparation: 15 min — Cooking: 10 min in the microwave + 15 min in the oven (200 °C)

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Floury potatoes (Russet, Maris Piper) | 4 large, even-sized |
| Fresh cheese, Boursin type (shallot & chive) | 150 g |
| Butter | 1 knob per potato |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- Microwave oven
- Conventional oven, grill or fan setting
- Fork or Parisian scoop for hollowing
- Bowl and fork for mashing

## 4. Preparation

1. Wash the potatoes without peeling them. Prick them all over with a fork.
2. Pre-cook in the microwave at full power: 8 to 10 min for four potatoes, turning them halfway. They should stay firm.
3. Let them cool for a few minutes, then cut them in half lengthwise.
4. Scoop out about half of the flesh of each half with a fork, keeping a wall of about 1 cm.
5. Mash the scooped-out flesh in a bowl, then mix with the fresh cheese, salt and pepper.
6. Divide the filling between the potato halves.
7. Place a knob of butter on each filled half.
8. Gratinate in the oven at 200 °C, grill setting, for 12 to 15 min until golden.

## 5. Variations

- Add grilled bacon bits or shredded ham to the filling for a complete version.
- Replace part of the fresh cheese with grated cheese (comté, gruyère) for a stronger gratin.
- Chop fresh chives over the potatoes at the end of cooking.

## 6. Key points

- Even-sized potatoes guarantee uniform cooking.
- Hollowing out too much weakens the wall: keep about 1 cm of flesh.
- Watch the gratin at the end of cooking, the grill browns quickly.
