## 1. Overview

A chicken basquaise prepared entirely in a single Dutch oven: the chicken is seared, the vegetables are confited in the cooking juices, with no transfers between several pots. The aromatic base sticks to the traditional trio of tomatoes, peppers and onion, lifted with Espelette pepper. A cast iron pot, which spreads the heat evenly, ensures gentle cooking that binds the sauce without constant watching.

**Serves 4** — Preparation: 15 min — Cooking: 45 min

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken thighs | 4 |
| Red peppers | 2 |
| Green pepper | 1 |
| Ripe tomatoes or canned peeled tomatoes | 4 (or 400 g) |
| Onion | 1 |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Espelette pepper | 1 tsp |
| Bay leaf | 1 |
| Fresh thyme | 2 sprigs |
| Tomato concentrate | 1 tsp |
| Diced Bayonne ham (optional) | 100 g |
| Black olives (optional) | 10 |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 4 to 5 L cast iron Dutch oven with lid
- Board and cook's knife
- Kitchen tongs to turn the thighs

## 4. Preparation

1. Seed the peppers and cut them into 1 cm strips. Slice the onion, chop the garlic, dice the tomatoes (keep them whole if canned).
2. Heat the olive oil in the pot over medium heat. Brown the chicken thighs on each side, 6 to 8 min in total, then set them aside on a plate.
3. Cook the onion and garlic for 3 min in the pot, without washing it. Add the peppers and Bayonne ham, continue for 5 min until the peppers start to soften.
4. Add the tomatoes, tomato concentrate, Espelette pepper, bay leaf and thyme. Season with salt and pepper, mix.
5. Nestle the thighs into the vegetables, skin side up. Cover and simmer over low heat for 30 to 35 min, until the meat comes off the bone with a fork.
6. Remove the lid for the last 5 minutes to thicken the sauce if it is still runny. Add the olives 5 min before the end of cooking.
7. Remove the bay leaf and thyme sprigs, adjust the seasoning. Serve straight from the pot.

## 5. Variations

- Replace the thighs with a farm chicken cut into eight pieces, extending the covered cooking time by 10 min.
- Add 1 tsp smoked paprika to the Espelette pepper for a woodier profile.
- Deglaze the pot with dry white wine (10 cl) after cooking the onion, before adding the tomatoes.
- Scatter chopped flat parsley over just before serving.

## 6. Key points

- Too high a heat makes the tomatoes stick to the bottom: keep the heat low throughout the simmering.
- The chicken should be browned, not cooked, at step 2: it cooks through while simmering.
- Espelette pepper mellows as it cooks: taste the sauce at the end before adding more.
- Leftovers keep 2 to 3 days in the fridge and gain flavour when reheated, over low heat and covered.
