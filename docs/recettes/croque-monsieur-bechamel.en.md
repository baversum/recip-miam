## 1. Overview

A croque monsieur topped with a comté and old-style mustard béchamel. The béchamel keeps the bread moist during storage, so the croque monsieur stays soft when reheated. Makes 2 meal boxes. Preparation: 30 minutes. Cooking: 15 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Crustless (or brioche) sandwich bread | 8 slices |
| White ham | 160 g |
| Grated comté and emmental (mix) | 120 g |
| Butter | 40 g |
| Flour | 2 tbsp |
| Whole milk | 25 cl |
| Old-style (grain) mustard | 1 tsp |
| Nutmeg | 1 pinch |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 saucepan
- 1 lidded frying pan
- 1 whisk
- 2 glass meal boxes

## 4. Preparation

1. Béchamel: melt 20 g of butter in the saucepan over medium heat, add the flour and stir for 1 minute to make a roux. Pour in the hot milk gradually, whisking. Add 1 tbsp of grated cheese, the mustard, nutmeg, salt and pepper. Leave to thicken 3 to 4 minutes over low heat.
2. Spread a thin layer of béchamel on 4 slices of bread. Place 1 slice of ham and 20 g of grated cheese on each, then close with the remaining slices.
3. Coat the top with a second layer of béchamel and sprinkle with grated cheese.
4. Melt the remaining butter in the pan over medium heat. Cook the croque monsieurs 4 to 5 minutes per side, covered so the cheese melts. Oven option: 10 minutes at 180 °C after brushing with melted butter.

## 5. Serving and storage

- Reheating: 2 minutes in the microwave at full power, or 5 minutes in the oven at 160 °C to bring the crunch back.
- Storage: up to 3 days in the fridge. The croque monsieurs can also be assembled and stored raw, then cooked the same day.

## 6. Variations

- Add pan-fried sliced mushrooms to the béchamel.
- Replace the comté with gruyère or cheddar.
