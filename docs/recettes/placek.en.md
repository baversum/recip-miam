## 1. Overview

A Polish apple cake: a soft brioche topped with apple slices and a cinnamon crumble, baked in the Cake Factory sharing-cakes programme. The 40 °C proof speeds up the brioche rise and gives a light crumb without a long wait at room temperature. Makes 8 slices. Preparation: 30 minutes. Proofing: 1 h 30. Baking: 45 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

### Brioche

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Flour | 300 g |
| Milk | 120 ml |
| Unsalted butter | 80 g |
| Sugar | 60 g |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Fresh yeast | 21 g |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |

### Filling

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Apples | 3 |

### Crumble

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Flour | 150 g |
| Sugar | 100 g |
| Cold unsalted butter | 90 g |
| Cinnamon | 1 tsp |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 stand mixer or 1 bowl for kneading by hand
- 1 round tin or the Cake Factory sharing-cake tin
- 1 Cake Factory or 1 oven

## 4. Brioche dough

1. Warm the milk to about 40 °C and dissolve the crumbled fresh yeast in it.
2. Mix the flour, sugar and salt in the mixer bowl. Add the eggs then the yeasted milk, and knead 10 minutes at medium speed.
3. Add the diced butter gradually, waiting for each addition to be incorporated. Knead 10 more minutes, until the dough is soft and pulls away from the sides.
4. Cover and let rise 1 h 30 at 40 °C (oven warmed then switched off, or proofing programme), until the dough has doubled.

## 5. Crumble and filling

1. Rub the flour, sugar, cinnamon and cold diced butter between your fingertips until sandy. Keep in the fridge.
2. Core the apples and cut them into thin slices.

## 6. Assembly and baking

1. Butter the tin and spread the dough about 2 cm thick.
2. Arrange the apple slices over the dough.
3. Crumble the topping over the apples in an even layer.
4. Bake 45 minutes at 180 °C (Cake Factory sharing-cakes programme), until the crumble is golden and the dough baked through.
5. Cool for 30 minutes before unmoulding.

## 7. Key points

- Fresh yeast dies above 45 °C: the milk must be warm, never hot.
- The butter goes in only once the dough is formed: adding it too early gives a greasy, sticky dough.
- Without a 40 °C proofer, let the dough rise 2 hours at room temperature, away from drafts.
- An overworked crumble turns greasy: rub quickly and keep the butter cold.

## 8. Variations

- Pears or apricots in season instead of apples.
- A few soaked sultanas scattered over the brioche before baking.
- Replace half the crumble sugar with brown sugar for a more caramelised taste.

## 9. Serving and storage

- Serve warm or at room temperature.
- Storage: 3 days under a cake dome at room temperature.
- Reheating: 5 minutes at 150 °C to restore the crumble's crunch.
