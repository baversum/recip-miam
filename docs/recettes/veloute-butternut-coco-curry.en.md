## 1. Overview

A thick, smooth soup with a coating texture, served in small glasses as an appetizer or as a starter. The butternut squash brings the melt-in-the-mouth body, the coconut milk the roundness. Makes 4 small glasses or 2 bowls. Preparation: 10 minutes. Cooking: 25 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Butternut squash flesh, diced | 700 g (net weight, peeled and seeded) |
| Yellow onion | 1 |
| Coconut milk | 20 cl |
| Vegetable stock | 50 cl (1 dissolved stock cube) |
| Olive oil | 1 tbsp |
| Hazelnuts | 2 tbsp |
| Curry powder | 1 pinch (optional) |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

The 700 g weight is net flesh, after peeling and seeding. A range of 550 to 800 g is fine: the stock is always adjusted to just cover, and the coconut milk never exceeds 20 cl for 700 g of flesh.

## 3. Equipment

- 1 pot or stockpot
- 1 stick blender
- 1 peeler

## 4. Preparation

1. Slice the onion. Cook it 2 to 3 minutes in the olive oil over medium heat, without colouring.
2. Peel the squash, remove the seeds and cut the flesh into 2 cm dice.
3. Add the dice to the pot, stir for 1 minute to coat.
4. Pour in hot stock to just cover: the liquid should just reach the top of the dice without covering them completely, otherwise the velouté stays runny.
5. Cover and cook 20 minutes over medium heat, checking halfway that nothing is sticking.
6. Test the cooking: a knife blade goes through the dice without resistance.
7. Blend with the coconut milk, salt, pepper and curry. The texture should coat: the velouté holds on the back of a spoon.

## 5. Serving

- Reheat over low heat before serving, without boiling.
- Toast the hazelnuts 5 minutes in the oven at 180 °C, crush them and scatter over just before serving.

## 6. Storage

- 48 hours in the fridge in a closed container.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months, without the hazelnuts.

## 7. Variations

- A pinch of ground ginger for a lift.
- A few drops of hazelnut oil when serving.
- Replace the butternut with a small Hokkaido pumpkin, no peeling needed.
