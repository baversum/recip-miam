## 1. Overview

A one-pot dish where the rice cooks directly in a broth flavoured with chorizo, peppers, red beans and corn. The chorizo fat diffuses into the rice while it cooks and keeps the dish moist after several days of storage; basmati rice stays nicely separate when reheated. Makes 3 meal boxes. Preparation: 15 minutes. Cooking: 25 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cooking chorizo, diced | 200 g |
| Basmati (or long grain) rice | 150 g |
| Diced red pepper | 1 |
| Diced yellow pepper | 1 |
| Sliced red onion | 1 small |
| Canned red kidney beans, drained | 240 g |
| Canned corn, drained | 150 g |
| Canned crushed tomatoes | 200 g |
| Chopped garlic | 2 cloves |
| Cumin | 1 tsp |
| Smoked paprika | 1 tsp |
| Cayenne pepper (optional) | 1/2 tsp |
| Chicken stock | 40 cl |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |
| Fresh coriander (cilantro) | for finishing |

A cooking chorizo works better than the dry snack-type chorizo, which hardens when cooked. Adjust the stock to the rice: if the grains are still firm after 20 minutes, add 5 cl of hot water and cook 5 more minutes, covered.

## 3. Equipment

- 1 Dutch oven or stockpot with a lid
- 3 glass meal boxes

## 4. Preparation

1. Cook the onion and garlic in the olive oil over medium heat for 3 minutes, without colouring.
2. Add the diced chorizo and brown it for 5 minutes so it releases its fat.
3. Add the peppers, cumin, smoked paprika and cayenne, season with salt and pepper, then cook for 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the rice and stir for 1 minute to coat it in fat and spices.
5. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, red beans, corn and stock.
6. Bring to the boil, lower the heat, cover and simmer 15 to 20 minutes, until the rice is cooked and the liquid absorbed.
7. Cool for 10 minutes, then divide between the 3 glass boxes.

## 5. Serving and storage

- Reheating: 3 to 4 minutes in the microwave at full power with 1 tbsp of water, stirring halfway, or over low heat in a pan with a splash of water.
- Fresh coriander survives neither cooking nor storage: keep it separate and scatter it over at mealtime.
- Storage: 4 to 5 days in the fridge.

## 6. Variations

- Replace the chorizo with pork strips or prawns, added at the end of cooking.
- Replace the cayenne with mild paprika for a milder version.
- Scatter grated cheddar over when reheating.
