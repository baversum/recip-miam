## 1. Overview

A poultry sauté in cider: the floured pieces give body to the sauce, the cider brings acidity, and the melting apples the sweetness that balances the dish. Cream is stirred in at the end to soften it. Serves 4. Preparation: 15 minutes. Cooking: 25 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken or turkey breasts | 800 g |
| Flour | 2 tbsp |
| Butter | 30 g |
| Onion | 1 |
| Apples | 3 |
| Dry cider | 25 to 30 cl |
| Thick cream | 10 cl |
| Thyme | 2 sprigs |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 lidded deep pan
- 1 board and 1 knife

## 4. Preparation

1. Slice the onion. Core the apples and cut them into quarters, without peeling.
2. Cut the poultry into pieces, season with salt and pepper, and dust with flour.
3. Melt the butter in the pan over medium heat and brown the poultry for 5 minutes, stirring, then set aside.
4. Cook the onion 3 minutes without colouring, add the apples and thyme, cook 5 minutes.
5. Deglaze with the cider, scraping the juices from the bottom. Return the poultry, cover and simmer 10 to 12 minutes over low heat, until the apples are melting.
6. Stir in the cream and cook 2 more minutes over low heat. Adjust the seasoning.

## 5. Key points

- The flour coats the poultry and naturally thickens the sauce: do not skip it.
- The apples break down if cooked too long: keep them in firm quarters, or crush them with a fork for a stewed-apple sauce.
- The cider reduces and loses its bitterness while cooking: taste the sauce before salting at the end.

## 6. Variations

- 1 tbsp calvados added with the cider.
- A few smoked bacon bits fried with the onion.
- Steamed potatoes or rice on the side.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve with rice, mash or steamed potatoes.
- Storage: 3 days in the fridge in an airtight container.
- Reheating: 5 to 7 minutes over low heat, without boiling or the cream will split.
