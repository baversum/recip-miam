## 1. Overview

A frittata with melting potatoes and chopped spring onions (scallions), cooked in a pan then browned under the grill. Pre-cooking the potatoes in water or steam guarantees a melting texture without extending the pan cooking time.

**Serves 4** — Preparation: 15 min — Cooking: 10 min (potatoes) + 15 min in the pan and under the grill

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Potatoes | 600 g |
| Spring onions (scallions), greens included | 4 |
| Eggs | 6 |
| Grated parmesan (optional) | 2 tbsp |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- Oven-safe frying pan (removable or metal handle)
- Large pot or steamer for the pre-cooking
- Bowl and fork to beat the eggs
- Platter or serving board for unmoulding

## 4. Preparation

1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into roughly 5 mm slices. Cook 10 min in salted boiling water or steam, until tender but still firm. Drain.
2. Slice the spring onions, whites and greens separately.
3. Beat the eggs in a bowl with the parmesan, salt and pepper.
4. Heat the olive oil in the pan over medium heat. Fry the potatoes and the white parts of the spring onions 8 to 10 min, stirring regularly, until lightly coloured. Add the green tops at the end.
5. Pour the beaten eggs over the potatoes, spread evenly and cook over low heat, covered, 6 to 8 min until the top is just set.
6. Slide the pan under the oven grill for 2 to 3 min to brown the surface.
7. Rest for 2 min, then slide the frittata onto a plate. Serve warm or cold.

## 5. Variations

- Add grilled bacon bits or shredded ham to the potatoes for a complete version.
- Fold in sliced peppers fried with the spring onions.
- Replace the parmesan with crumbled goat cheese, added at the end of the pan cooking.
- Add a few drops of fresh cream to the beaten eggs for a softer texture.

## 6. Key points

- A non-stick or well-oiled pan makes unmoulding easy without breaking.
- Too high a heat browns the bottom before the centre is cooked: keep the heat low after adding the eggs.
- Watch the time under the grill, the surface browns quickly.
- The frittata reheats poorly at high temperature: eat it warm or cold, or reheat it gently in the oven at 120 °C.
