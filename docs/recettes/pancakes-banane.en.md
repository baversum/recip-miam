## 1. Overview

High-protein pancakes with no added sugar: the banana sweetens the batter naturally, Greek yoghurt brings the moistness and baking powder the lift. The thick batter makes 6 to 8 small pancakes for 2 people. Preparation: 10 minutes. Cooking: 10 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ripe banana | 1 |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Greek yoghurt | 200 g |
| Flour | 40 g |
| Baking powder | 1/2 tsp |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 bowl
- 1 fork or stick blender
- 1 non-stick pan
- 1 small ladle

## 4. Preparation

1. Mash the banana with a fork into a rough purée.
2. Add the eggs and yoghurt, mix until homogeneous.
3. Fold in the flour and baking powder without overworking: the batter should stay thick.
4. Rest the batter 10 minutes so the baking powder starts working.
5. Heat the pan over medium-low heat, with a thin film of oil if needed.
6. Drop small ladles of batter, spread slightly, cook 2 to 3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface, flip, then cook 1 to 2 more minutes.

## 5. Key points

- The batter is thicker than a classic pancake batter: do not try to smooth it completely.
- Too high a heat browns the surface before the centre cooks: keep the heat medium-low.
- A very ripe banana sweetens the batter more and caramelises faster.

## 6. Variations

- 1/2 tsp cinnamon or cocoa in the batter.
- A few blueberries dropped on each pancake before flipping.
- Pan-fried banana slices as a topping.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve warm, with a little Greek yoghurt or fresh fruit.
- Storage: 48 hours in the fridge once cooked.
- Reheating: 30 seconds in the microwave or a few minutes in the toaster.
