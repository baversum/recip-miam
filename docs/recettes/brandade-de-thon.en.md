## 1. Overview

A tuna brandade gratin: potatoes mashed with milk and butter, mixed with flaked tuna and persillade, gratinated under a golden breadcrumb topping. The potato brings the softness, the tuna the texture, and 15 to 20 minutes in a 180 °C oven binds it all. Serves 4. Preparation: 20 minutes. Cooking: 25 minutes (potatoes) + 20 minutes in the oven.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Potatoes | 1.2 kg |
| Milk | 20 cl |
| Butter | 40 g |
| Tuna in water (1 large can) | 170 g drained |
| Persillade (chopped garlic and parsley) | 1 tbsp |
| Mild or Espelette chilli | 1 pinch |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Breadcrumbs | 2 tbsp |
| Salt | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 large pot
- 1 food mill or 1 fork
- 1 gratin dish

## 4. Preparation

1. Peel the potatoes, cut them into chunks and cook 25 minutes in salted water, until a knife blade goes through without resistance. Drain.
2. Mash the potatoes with a food mill or fork with the hot milk and butter, until you get a soft but not runny purée.
3. Drain and flake the tuna, fold it into the purée with the persillade, chilli, olive oil and salt. Mix without overworking.
4. Transfer to the gratin dish, smooth the surface and sprinkle with breadcrumbs.
5. Bake 15 to 20 minutes at 180 °C, until the surface is golden.

## 5. Key points

- The brandade should stay soft: add the milk gradually, absorption varies with the potato variety.
- The tuna is folded in at the end: overworked, it turns pasty.
- Breadcrumbs mixed with a drizzle of olive oil brown better than dry crumbs.

## 6. Variations

- A squeeze of lemon juice and the zest of half a lemon in the brandade.
- Confited cherry tomatoes laid on the surface before gratinating.
- Replace the tuna with traditional salt cod brandade.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve with a peppery, well-dressed green salad to cut through the softness.
- Storage: 48 hours in the fridge wrapped in film.
- Reheating: 15 minutes at 160 °C.
