## 1. Overview

Small cheese choux served as an appetizer, the gougère is the savoury version of choux pastry. This comté recipe makes about 30 mini 2 cm gougères, for 4 to 6 people with drinks. Preparation: 25 minutes. Cooking: 15 to 18 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter | 75 g |
| Water | 10 cl |
| Milk | 5 cl |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| Flour | 90 g |
| Eggs | 3 (to adjust) |
| Grated comté | 100 g |
| Espelette pepper | 1 pinch (optional) |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 saucepan
- 1 wooden spatula
- 1 bowl
- 1 baking sheet lined with baking paper
- 1 piping bag or 2 small spoons

## 4. Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Pour the water, milk, diced butter and salt into a saucepan. Bring to the boil: the butter must be completely melted at the first boil.
3. Off the heat, add all the flour at once and stir vigorously with the spatula.
4. Return to low heat for 1 minute to dry the dough: it forms a ball that pulls away from the sides.
5. Transfer to a bowl and let it cool for 2 minutes, so the eggs do not cook on contact.
6. Beat in the eggs one by one, waiting for each to be absorbed before adding the next. Add the third egg gradually: the dough is ready when it is soft, glossy and forms a ribbon that folds back when you lift the spatula.
7. Add the grated comté and Espelette pepper, then mix.
8. PIPE: form 2 cm mounds on the baking sheet, well spaced, with a piping bag or two spoons.

## 5. Baking

- Bake 15 to 18 minutes at 200 °C, without opening the door during baking.
- The gougères are done when puffed and golden, and they sound slightly hollow when tapped.

## 6. Storage and reheating

- Cooled gougères: 24 hours at room temperature in a non-airtight box, or 3 days in the fridge.
- Reheating: 5 minutes at 150 °C, which restores the day's crispness.
- Can be frozen once cooked, reheated 8 minutes at 150 °C without thawing.

## 7. Variations

- Emmental or gruyère instead of comté.
- A few chopped thyme leaves in the dough.
- Classic version with 4 to 5 cm gougères: bake 20 to 25 minutes.
