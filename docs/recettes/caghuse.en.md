## 1. Overview

Caghuse is a slow-braised pork leg roast with onions and prunes, built on cider vinegar and baked in an oven started cold. The vinegar's acidity and the prunes' sweetness balance the softness of the confit onions, and the gentle oven cooking makes the roast meltingly tender. Serves 6. Preparation: 25 minutes. Cooking: 1 hour.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Pork leg roast (rouelle) | 1.2 kg |
| Large onions | 6 |
| Potatoes | 1 kg |
| Butter | 100 g |
| Cider vinegar | 15 cl |
| Cider | 15 cl |
| Prunes | 150 g |
| Bay leaves | 2 |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 cast iron Dutch oven with lid, oven-safe
- 1 board and 1 knife
- 1 small saucepan for the vinegar

## 4. Preparation

1. Peel and quarter the onions.
2. Place two thirds of the onions at the bottom of the pot with the butter. Cover with the whole roast. Brown the roast with the onions 10 minutes over medium heat, season with salt and pepper.
3. Meanwhile, peel the potatoes and cut them into chunks.
4. Cover the roast with the remaining third of onions and the potatoes.
5. Bring the vinegar to the boil in a saucepan, then pour the vinegar and the cider into the pot.
6. Season again with salt and pepper. Add the prunes and bay leaves.
7. Cover and put in a cold oven, 1 hour at 190 °C. If time allows, baste regularly, turning the roast and the onions.

## 5. Key points

- The oven starts cold: the gradual rise in temperature avoids thermal shock on the cast iron and lets the meat relax.
- Vinegar boiled before adding loses its harshness: do not pour it in cold.
- Rest the roast 10 minutes before slicing: the juices redistribute through the meat.

## 6. Variations

- Pork chops or shoulder instead of the leg roast, extending the cooking by 15 minutes.
- Two apples quartered and added with the prunes.
- A glass of calvados flambed over the meat before putting it in the oven.

## 7. Serving and storage

- Serve straight from the pot, with the potatoes and onions.
- Storage: 2 to 3 days in the fridge; freezes well without the potatoes.
- Reheating: 20 minutes at 150 °C, covered, with a splash of cider.
