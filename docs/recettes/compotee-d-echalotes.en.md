## 1. Overview

Shallots slowly melted in butter then deglazed with white wine, this compote accompanies pan-seared scallops, pan-fried white fish or grilled meat. The wine reduction brings the acidity that lifts mild products. Quantity for 2 people, as an accompaniment to 8 to 10 scallops. Preparation: 10 minutes. Cooking: 30 minutes.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Shallots | 8 |
| Unsalted butter | 30 g |
| Dry white wine | 10 cl |
| Flat parsley | a few sprigs |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

## 3. Equipment

- 1 heavy-bottomed saucepan
- 1 paring knife

## 4. Preparation

1. Finely slice the shallots.
2. Melt them 15 to 20 minutes over low heat in the butter with a pinch of salt, without colouring. The shallots should turn translucent, pearly and melting.
3. Deglaze with the white wine, scraping the bottom of the pan to release the juices.
4. Reduce over low heat for about 10 minutes, until almost no liquid remains. The compote is ready when it looks glossy and slightly syrupy, with no water at the bottom.
5. Off the heat, season with salt and pepper. The seasoning should be assertive: the compote lifts very mild accompaniments.
6. Keep off the heat. Chopped parsley goes in at the last moment, never during cooking or storage: cooked or stored, it darkens and loses its flavour.

## 5. Reuse and reheating

- Storage: one night in the fridge in a closed container, without parsley.
- Reheating: very low heat, 5 to 8 minutes covered, with 2 to 3 tbsp of water or a splash of white wine to loosen the texture.
- Finishing touch when serving: a knob of butter swirled in off the heat, for gloss and smoothness. Cream is not recommended: it masks the fruitiness of the reduction and weighs the side dish down.

## 6. Plating with scallops

1. Pat the scallops dry, season with salt and pepper. Take them out of the fridge 15 minutes before cooking.
2. Sear 1 minute per side in foaming butter, no longer: the centre must stay pearly.
3. Plate a spoonful of warm compote, set the scallops on top, scatter with chopped parsley and fleur de sel.

## 7. Tips

- A slightly rubbery texture after reduction is fixed when reheating: cooking covered over low heat with a little water softens the shallots in a few minutes.
- The compote freezes very well for up to 3 months.
