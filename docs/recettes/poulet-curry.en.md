## 1. Overview

A dish of diced poultry simmered in a coconut milk and fresh cream sauce flavoured with curry, with red pepper and tomato purée. The sauce is smooth, slightly sweet and medium in strength; the pepper brings crunch and colour. Serve it with rice or flatbread for a complete everyday meal. Serves 4, with 15 minutes of preparation and 25 minutes of cooking.

## 2. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken or turkey fillets or breasts | 700 g |
| Coconut milk | 400 ml |
| Thick fresh cream | 20 cl |
| Red pepper | 1 |
| Tomato purée | 2 tbsp |
| Onion | 1 |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Fresh ginger | 20 g (a 2 cm piece) |
| Red or yellow curry paste | 2 tbsp |
| Vegetable or coconut oil | 1 tbsp |
| Sugar | 1 tsp |
| Fresh coriander (cilantro) | a few sprigs |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

As a substitute for the curry paste: 1 tbsp curry powder and 1 tsp ground cumin. The coconut milk and cream amounts tolerate ±20 % without affecting the texture of the sauce.

## 3. Equipment

- Heavy-bottomed Dutch oven or deep pan
- Board and knife
- Fine grater for the ginger
- Wooden spoon

## 4. Preparation

1. Finely dice the onion and garlic, grate the ginger.
2. Seed the red pepper and cut it into strips.
3. Cut the poultry into roughly 3 cm dice, season with salt and pepper.
4. Heat the oil in the pot over medium heat and brown the poultry dice for 5 minutes, stirring, then set aside.
5. Cook the onion, garlic and ginger for 3 minutes in the pot, without colouring.
6. Add the curry paste and cook for 1 minute, stirring.
7. Add the red pepper and cook for 3 minutes.
8. Add the tomato purée and stir for 1 minute.
9. Pour in the coconut milk, scraping the juices from the bottom of the pot.
10. Return the poultry, add the sugar, then simmer 12 to 15 minutes over low heat, covered.
11. Stir in the fresh cream and cook 5 more minutes over low heat.
12. Adjust the seasoning with salt, pepper and sugar.
13. Scatter chopped coriander over just before serving.

## 5. Serving and storage

- Serve with basmati or Thai rice, or flatbread such as naan.
- The coriander goes in at the very last moment, off the heat.
- Can be eaten straight away or later.
- Storage: 3 days in the fridge in an airtight container.
- Reheating: 5 to 7 minutes over low heat stirring, or 2 minutes in the microwave at medium power.
- Freezing not recommended: the fresh cream may split when thawing.

## 6. Variations

- Replace the fresh cream with Greek yoghurt → lighter version, stirred in off the heat.
- Add 1 tbsp peanut butter at step 9 → thicker sauce with a nutty taste.
- Add 1 finely chopped fresh chilli with the pepper → spicier dish.
- Replace the poultry with prawns → cook only 5 minutes after adding the coconut milk.
- Cook the rice in half coconut milk, half water → fragrant side dish.
